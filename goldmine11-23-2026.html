Researcher settings

All scale readings are fixed in advance, so every participant sees the same numbers. Each robot’s readings average exactly its true weight (Sandra 1.3 g, Minhee 4.6 g, David 5.2 g; scale error at most ±1 g; dial 0–10 g). Deception: on paired trips Sandra’s gold is secretly increased by 1.0 g. The 8-trip version adds six Sandra readings that keep her average at 1.3 g, so only certainty differs between versions. All parameters and the exact readings are included in the exported data.
